Elite Island Movers

A simple, fast one-page website for a local moving company in Victoria, BC — services, straightforward pricing and contact, with local-business structured data.

Public repoHTMLShipped 2025
View on GitHub

What it is

A small local business needed a page that loads fast and answers the three questions customers ask: what do you do, what does it cost, how do I reach you.

What I built

  • A single responsive HTML/CSS page with services, pricing and contact sections.
  • MovingCompany structured data (Schema.org) for local search.
  • A rotating hero image and a simple, readable colour system.
