# Elevatron

A fully-cited concept study: could a magnetically-pinched, laser-coupled beam act as both conductor and structure for reaching space? The honest answer is no — but the physics underneath is real.

## What it is

Elevatron started as a conversation-level idea and became an experiment in honest citation: every load-bearing claim is tied to a real source, and every place where the popular framing outruns the physics is flagged.

## What I built

-   Eight chapters from abstract to conclusions, with derivations of the Bennett relation, Z-pinch behaviour and MHD instabilities.
-   A feasibility analysis — energy budget, instability growth, atmospheric breakdown — with a clear verdict.
-   A graduate-style lab chapter of safe, low-cost benchtop demonstrations of the real physics.
-   A BibTeX bibliography and an annotated reading list.
